Do they miss it?

The loop every route on this site leads to, in five panels: ship it, give it away, take it away, ask, then charge or ship again.

startups.engineer, 20 October 2026, a first strip for Dinis to review. Every line is the storyboard's own words unless marked from_finder, in which case it is a string from finder.json word for word, and the build checks it. The loop is the operating model of the newsroom article 'the most important question is whether they miss it'.
