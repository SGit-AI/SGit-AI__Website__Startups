## 5. 4. Ask

*punchline*

> **Do they miss it?**

- **Ana** (think, thinking) says: “Two of the five wrote back the same day.”
- **The Guide** (point, determined) says: “That is the signal.”

A shrug means no why now. Somebody asking for it back is the only thing that earns the right to charge.

*Source: newsroom.sgit.ai, the most important question is whether they miss it (not a quote)*

## 6. 5. If they ask for it back, charge. If they shrug, ship again

*scene*

- **Ana** (arms-up, excited) says: “A price they are happy to pay, and profitable for me.”
- **The Guide** (stand, happy) says: “If they shrug, there is no why now. Back to one, with better information.”

Props: tag (on demand)

Two questions, and people answer only the first: is the price one they are happy to pay, and is it profitable for you. Pay on demand is usually the healthier shape.
