The cast

7 characters, one per way in on the finder's spectrum plus the voice of the site and the agent that runs the interview. Three are shared with the book's cast, look unchanged, so the family of sites reads the same; four are this site's own. Every look is data in cast.json, drawn by the kit; nothing is an image.

The model sheet

Every character in every mood and every pose the kit knows, so a storyboard's author can pick by eye.

The cast is a proposal for Dinis (cast.json, what). Characters use the pronouns their cast entry gives. A character's badge (explorer, villager, town planner) is a centre of gravity, not a box, as the book describes it.
