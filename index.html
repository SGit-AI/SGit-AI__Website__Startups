Find your startup. Then ship it.

An interview that helps you zoom in on what to ship, to whom, by when; and the substrate that makes shipping it cheap enough to do this week.

Most people have a good idea. What they are not doing is shipping. The finder is a who-done-it of questions that ends in your own description of what to focus on, with the route picked by a rule you can read. It is not a verdict on your idea; it does not pass judgement. The answer may be: ship the smallest version to five people by Friday. It may be: go old school, raise first.

Where are you?

The finder works along the whole spectrum. The first question is which of these you are; it decides what comes next.

blank

I do not have a startup yet

Start from zero: what you want to build, how you want to work, what you already have.

many

I have several ideas and cannot pick

Bring focus: the one that can ship first, and what the others wait for.

one

I have one idea and have not shipped it

The usual case. A good idea, and every reason in the book why it is not ready yet.

plan

I have a plan, maybe a company, and I want focus

A plan already written; we zoom in on the thing to ship next and what to stop doing.

shipping

I am shipping and want the next step

Give it away, take it away, find out whether they miss it, then charge.

What this site does not claim

  • The finder has not been run with anyone. The questions and rules are a first reading of one memo; the first interview is the interviewer's first issue.
  • It does not judge your idea. By decision; the right startup is the one you are passionate about.
  • sgit.ai's startups pages still exist. The section moved here on 20 October 2026; until the old pages carry a notice, both exist.
  • The review folder is partial and the vault does not exist. The reality file says exactly what does.
