Walk the interview

The 7 stages, in the browser. Your answers stay in this browser (nothing is sent anywhere); the route on the right is picked by the rules as you answer, with the rule named; the last stage writes your record as markdown to copy or save. For the conversation the page cannot have, take the record to a model with the prompt.

The component is assets/components/sg-finder/, three files in the shape coding.sgit.ai documents, on a base component kept in this repository; the rules it applies are finder-rules.js, the same module the release test runs. Answers are kept under startups.finder.v1 in this browser's storage until you clear them.
