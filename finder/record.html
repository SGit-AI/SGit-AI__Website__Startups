The record

The finder's output: your startup, in your own words. One page, with the thing in a sentence, the first user, what exists, the smallest thing you will ship and when, the five people and the take-away date, the money, the route with the rule that picked it, what you are not doing, and your focus line. The next record supersedes it and says what changed.

The template

# <name>'s startup, in their own words

*Finder record. Date: <date>. Interviewer: <Claude, ChatGPT, the page at startups.sgit.ai/finder/interview.html, or a person>.
Finder version <finder.json version>. Status: a first record; the next one supersedes it and says what changed.*

## Where I am

<The entry: one of the five, in the person's words.>

## The thing, in one sentence

<Who uses it, to do what.>

## The first user, and what they do on day one

<One person, named or by role, and the first thing they do.>

## What exists today

<Nothing, a demo, a prototype, or something a stranger can use. The honest state.>

## The smallest thing I will ship, and when

<The smallest version a stranger could use, and the date it is in their hands.>

## The five people

<By name or role. How I give it to them. When and how I take it away. What missing it looks like.>

## The money

<What they would pay, in what shape, and whether I need outside money to reach the first paying user.>

## The route

<The route's title, the rule that fired and why, in the finder's words. If I disagree, what I would change.>

## What I am not doing, for now

- <one line each, dated where it matters>

## My focus, in my own words

<The sentence I would say to a friend who asked what I am working on.>

## Next

1. <action, date>
2. <action, date>
3. <action, date>

*Answers marked (from documents) were taken from material I supplied and confirmed. Answers marked (drafted) were
proposed by the interviewer from my other answers and corrected by me.*

finder-record.md as a file. The interview page writes the same shape from your answers; the prompt tells a model to fill it.

Examples

None published yet. The first will be Dinis’s own, as the memo says: "maybe I’ll show some examples, I’ll do it myself". It is the interviewer’s first issue. A record is published only with its owner’s say-so, under admin/content/finder/examples/, and says whose it is and how it was run.

Keeping yours

A record is yours. The page keeps your answers in your browser and nowhere else; a model keeps them in the conversation. Save the markdown as a file, and start the next interview from it: the point of the record is that it can be checked later against what you did.
