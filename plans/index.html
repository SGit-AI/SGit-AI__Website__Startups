Business plans to build on sgit

Businesses we think should exist on top of sgit, each written up as a vault in enough detail for somebody else to start. 8 plans as of 2026-10-20, each with the plan as an app and as documents, the numbers marked as hypotheses, the facts with their sources, and prototypes enough to serve the first customer. We are looking for business partners to build them. Where nobody does, we may build one ourselves. Either way the plan is public, on the same terms as the code.

The plans live in their vaults on sgit.ai and are linked, not copied: every plan's page there prints its public read key, and a vault is right where a copy would drift. This page is generated from admin/content/plans.json, one row per plan. If you have just found your startup and it is one of these, the plan is the head start.

How to take one

  1. Open it. Every plan's page on sgit.ai prints its public read key. Open it in the browser with nothing installed, or clone it with sgit clone.
  2. Read the one-page version. Every plan starts at plan/00-START-HERE.md, with a reading order after it.
  3. Test the numbers. Every price and every hour is a first guess, and every plan says so. The first ten customers are the test.
  4. Keep what you build. The plans are offered on the same terms as the code behind them: take it, run it, change it.
  5. Send back what broke. It is the one thing we ask, and it makes the next plan better.

What every plan contains

The one-page versionThe business, why now, what you sell, the numbers and why it is buildable, on one page.
Facts and assumptions, kept apartFacts link to their sources. Assumptions are labelled, with the arithmetic shown so they can be redone.
The architecture and the serviceHow it is built on vaults, what the customer gets, and what the operator does not do.
The model, with a calculatorPrices, costs, margins and break-even, with the inputs on sliders in the app.
Go-to-market and the first ninety daysWho buys first, the free thing that opens the door, and what done means at each stage.
Investors, risks and open questionsThe case for backing it, what could sink it, and what is not known yet.
PrototypesEnough to serve the first customer: playbooks, specifications, set-up steps and worked examples.
PUBLIC.mdThe rules the vault is published under: nothing private, no write token, and an app that asks for no permissions.
