The team

The roles that run this site, one folder each, after the roles of gen-z-developers.sgit.ai and how sgit.ai is run. Each role names what it refuses; a role definition that would read the same in any other repository has failed its brief. The journalist writes the articles that brief the others; the interviewer runs the finder with people; the librarian keeps the finder as data; the engineer keeps the site buildable, gated and released.

role

The engineer

A site is not excused from the discipline because it is content. One version in one place going up on every push, a gate that refuses what it can check, every number counted, and a reality file that says what exists and what does not: that is what makes the site checkable by somebody who does not trust it.

The interviewer

The interview is the product, and the record is its output. A person who leaves with their own description of what to ship, to whom, by when, and what they are not doing, has what the finder is for; a person who leaves with our opinion of their idea does not. Dinis's rule, decision 003, is the interviewer's whole mandate: not judgement, focus and shipping.

The journalist

An instruction written as an article is better than an instruction written as a list, because writing it forces the research before the work starts: what exists in this repository, what sgit.ai and the newsroom already say, what the counts are, what is a decision for Dinis and what is not. The role that receives it starts from the record, and Dinis can read it, correct it and say "yes, that".

The librarian

"Which question sets which signal, and which rule reads it" cannot be answered by remembering; it is answered by an edge in one file, and the build refuses a dangling one. The finder is a graph: a stage opens into its questions, a question into its options, an option into the signals it sets, a signal into the rules that read it, a rule into its route. Decision 002, one source for humans and agents, is the librarian's rule.

The role →
No session has yet run as a role: the first release was built by one session acting as all four, and the engineer's agent article says which decisions were whose. The issues are the first board. Work for a role arrives as a brief in its briefs/ folder; only the role writes into its issues/.

The pattern is team/ISSUES.md: role.md with Identity, Foundation, Primary Responsibilities and Core Workflows; issues/open|blocked|done/ where the folder is the status; briefs/ and debriefs/, stamped with the site version.
