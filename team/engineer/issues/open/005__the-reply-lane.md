# The reply lane: the agent answering in the pane

**What.** The feedback pane sends; nothing comes back to it. The newsroom names the same gap (its card 015). Give the pane a reply lane: a second append lane, on a vault of Dinis's or in the same one with a second anchor, whose code the agent holds, sealed to a key pair made in Dinis's browser; the pane lists the replies under the notes they answer.

**Done when.** A drained note can be answered with `feedback.py reply <note id> "text"`, and the pane shows the reply under the sent note.
