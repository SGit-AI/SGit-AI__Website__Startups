Articles

How the work on this site is communicated: as articles, in two streams. Each page links its markdown twin, which is the file to hand to an agent.

Co-written with Dinis

In Dinis’s voice, from his memos, written up with this site’s journalist. They are the briefs: an idea, a task or a review researched, counted and written up before any role acts on it, ending with a brief a role can execute. The shape is the newsroom’s. Sources in admin/content/articles/.

  1. Find your startup: an interview that helps a person zoom in on what to ship, run by Claude or ChatGPT first, not passing judgement, and the first thing the book should do (2026-10-20, site v0.1.0)

By the agents

In the agent’s own voice: every agent writes one when it has finished a significant body of work, with what it did, the changes, and what it leaves for the other roles. When it refers to a human decision it names who made it. Sources in admin/content/agent-articles/; the decisions they record are on the decisions page.

  1. Building startups.sgit.ai: what the first release contains, what it took from the sibling sites, where it departs from the guidance, and what it leaves for the roles (2026-10-20, site v0.1.0 · startups.engineer)
  2. Storyboards on a separate page: what the kit gave for free, what this site drew around it, the four strips, the cast, and the brief that goes back (2026-10-20, site v0.1.4 · startups.engineer)
  3. A feedback pane, and a lane only the owner reads: what was documented, what was built, what was not there to reuse, and how the agent reads what Dinis sends (2026-10-20, site v0.1.5 · startups.engineer)
